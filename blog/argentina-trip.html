Family in Pacheco Argentina

22 April 2014

It has been a bit quiet around here as I’ve been traveling with my family in Argentina and haven’t had much time to blog. A wedding, a double baptism, and a case of chicken pox later–it was quite a trip! I should hopefully have a bit more bandwidth over the next weeks to to update everyone on what I’ve been up to with my recent sessions, but in the meantime I wanted to share a few images from the last days with my adorable nephews and Argentinian family.

With the exception of a couple of minutes where i got the kids to pose on the ground we didn’t do any formal sessions so everything has a bit more photo journalistic feel than my normal style. Also, although I normally edit in color these images felt better in black and white to me for whatever reason. Anyway–here are my favorites! Enjoy :)
