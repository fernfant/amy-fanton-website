Hermione Harbutt 2015 Collection

15 December 2015

So beyond thrilled to see some of my work featured on Love My Dress today — the gorgeous Hermione Harbutt 2015 collection of nature-inspired headpieces and hair vines!

Earlier this summer I had the immense pleasure of photographing the 2015 collection for the insanely talented accessory designer, Hermione Harbutt . I’m not really sure where to begin describing the collection except to say that Hermione’s designs are simply the most beautiful I have literally ever laid eyes (and hands!) on.

The exquisite intricacy and delicacy of her pieces, her attention to detail, and the quality of craftsmanship are simply unmatched, and I am completely obsessed with the feminine and organic feel to her designs! We photographed the collection at the gorgeous Westonbirt estate in the Cotwolds where there seems to be reason to “ooh”

or “aah” just around every corner! The “Bride’s” stunning accessories were matched with beautiful ethereal gowns by Katya Katya Shehurina and Harriet Wilde shoes . I feel so honored to be trusted by Hermione to create imagery that reflected the beauty of her work and I hope you love the images as much as I do!!!

And as if it doesn’t get better than being asked to photograph so much pretty–I’m so pleased to let you know all know that images from the shoot have been published on Love My Dress and you can have a look at even more of the prettiness HERE . (Yay!) I shot these images using a combination of digital and medium format film.

Fortunately I was able to match them quite closely using Mastin labs so I’m super happy about that too! :) I’m so hoping I get to do some more Westonbirt Wedding Photography in the next year!
