Weddings

London Wedding Photographer in Turks & Caicos

13 February 2015

OK guys, I apologize in advance for such an image heavy post. I just couldn’t help myself. I know most people think of me as specializing in family photography, maternity photography and engagement photography–but tonight I have something special for you guys–a wedding!!! And what happens when you have a beautiful bride, a madly in love couple and a dreamy tropical wedding location?

You get the most stunningly beautiful wedding photographs, of course!!! I flew from London to Turks & Caicos back in November to help these New Yorkers (now technically Texans!) with their wedding photography on their special day. It was an intimate wedding with a modern feel and involved a lot of dancing, cigar rolling, and marshmallow roasting.

Goodies from places special to both the Bride and the Groom—Ohio, New York, Germany, and Texas–filled goodie bags and were sprinkled in details throughout the event. My favorite detail was the “message in a bottle”–such a cute touch! It was a wonderful break from the chilly weather here and during the trip I even managed to sneak in a beach day myself!

Thank you so much Sarah and Max for sharing your special day with me! I hope these wedding images warm you up a little in this cold weather!
