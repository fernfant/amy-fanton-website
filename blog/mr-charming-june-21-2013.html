Mr. Charming

25 June 2013

If you think you recognize this little man’s adorable face, you are probably right–he’s a little baby supermodel-making appearances on billboards internationally for big names like GAP, Paul Frank, Carters & Target! And as if he couldn’t be any more cute (and the bow tie doesn’t hurt) – little Matthew also has the sweetest, most charming personality. The first image is Matthew’s response to “Where are your dimples?”…I mean really?? Too much. I’ll take that boy any day!!! :)
