London Summer Family Photography Session

22 July 2014

Ok guys–So I’ve just joined a blog circle with a group of photographer friends! For those of you who aren’t familiar with what a blog circle is, each month we will all be posting an image in response to a theme along with a link to another photographer’s interpretation of the same theme, so as a reader you can circle through all of our blogs simply by clicking through the links in each person’s post.

And what summer would be complete with out a family field trip to a farm for some strawberry picking???!! Although I live in a big city, there is no doubt I’m a country girl at heart and this weekend I rallied the troops for a little country excursion and a mini summer family photography session. After picking strawberries we also made our way to fields of blackberries, raspberries, gooseberries, carrots and sunflowers!

The real excitement came when my son decided he wanted to bring his strawberries home in his newly discovered pockets rather than the basket that was meant to carry them. Eek that will be a bit of laundry tonight. I have the fondest memories of picking strawberries with my own mother when I was a child and I’m so looking forward to building these same memories with my own children.
