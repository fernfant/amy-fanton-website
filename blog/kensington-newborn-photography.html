Newborn & Maternity

Kensington London Newborn Photography Session

21 July 2014

This gorgeous family recently welcomed a beautiful baby girl into their family and invited me to their home to capture some of their first special moments all together. I’ve been doing quite a bit of newborn photography work lately and I have to say that I love the challenge of trying to capture the intimate, quiet moments with a new baby in a client’s home, even if things aren’t actually so quiet and peaceful in person!

I also especially look forward to newborn photography sessions when there is already another a child in the family because it means I get to do some larger family photography, some sibling photography, and some toddler photography all in one newborn photo session. Anyway, there is always a lot of talk about “pregnancy glow” but I think these parents are absolutely radiating. Here are some of my favorites from our session. Enjoy!
