An Elegant Argentinian Bridal Shoot

5 May 2015

I’ve done my own little blog post on the elegant Argentinian bridal shoot — pop over and take a look at some of my favourites from this dreamy day!

I have something really special for you guys today–an bridal inspiration shoot from our trip in Argentina back in February that was just published in Style Me Pretty! Since my husband is Argentinian, I have a bit of fascination with the old world elegance of Buenos Aires. Argentina is a country with such a rich history and strolling down the streets of San Telmo or San Isidro you get a glimpse into it’s past with the gold chandeliers, classic colonial architecture and leafy cobblestone streets.

I worked with a stellar team on this one and am so pleased with how it turned out. While I was in BA I was fortunate enough to photograph another wedding and should have those images up on the blog soon. Both shoots makes me want to hop on a plane back to the southern hemisphere to soak up more of this prettiness.

If you’re planning a destination wedding in Buenos Aires give me a call I’d love to help you with your wedding photography (and as you can see we know a few great spots ;) ) Otherwise I’ll be counting down the days till next year! :) Enjoy
