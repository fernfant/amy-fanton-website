Engagements

London Proposal Photography in Kensington

29 July 2014

This definitely has to be *the* most special engagement photography session I’ve done so far–a proposal followed by a surprise engagement session with two beautiful people who are madly in love–How do I get so lucky?!??! The incredibly thoughtful and romantic groom-to-be contacted me several weeks before his trip to London where he planned on popping the question and wanted me to document the happy moment with some surprise engagement photography.

Every detail was well thought out and he chose the most perfect setting for the proposal– the Changing of the Guard in front of Buckingham Palace. We selected an exact place where I would wait and watch it unfold. I was so excited I was giddy and there were definitely tears of joy. After the proposal, we walked through St.

James Park to take some engagement photos in a few iconic locations of London to commemorate the city where she said yes. Thanks so much you two for sharing such a special moment with me and congratulations on your engagement!!!
