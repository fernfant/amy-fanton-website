In Love In London

12 February 2014

Happy (almost) Valentines Day!!! I’m so excited to share the images from this London Anniversary Photography session to start getting everyone in a lovey-dovey kind of mood! Since it’s looking a bit grey around London right now, we decided to do a London landmark photo shoot and use some beautiful iconic London landmarks as our scenery.

This lovely couple just marked their 6 month anniversary and it was such a pleasure spending the session together to celebrate the milestone–they are so obviously in love and the warm fuzzies they emit are a little contagious. Not to mention the fact that they are gorgeous and were dressed perfectly for the occasion!

We shot this anniversary photo session the same way I would normally shoot an engagement photo session–the couple spending the hour or so really connecting and loving on each other. I really like the idea of using an urban landscape during this season since it’s still a few months before the beauty of the London Parks and Gardens peak.

Also, word on the street is that this couple might be relocating to Miami in a few months so a London Landmark Photo Session is the perfect way to commemorate their time here. There were literally too many images to choose from so I am just posting my favorite 10. Swooooooon….
