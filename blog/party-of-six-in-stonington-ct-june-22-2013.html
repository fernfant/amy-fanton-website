Family

Party of Six in Stonington

26 June 2013

So I’m not going to lie–I was a little intimidated about doing this shoot when I found out Mom is a super talented wedding photographer herself! Shooting a family with more than three children was also a first for me, and it was fantastic–it seemed like every time I turned there was something fun going on for me to capture, and I love how the dynamics in the family play out in the images!

Such a sweet, gentle, and loving family–makes me want to have a big family of my own! (but we’ll take it one at a time for now ;) )
