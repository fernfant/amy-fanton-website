Newborn & Maternity

London Autumn Maternity Shoot

2 December 2013

This mother is expecting her second baby any day now, and I have to say I don’t think I’ve ever seen someone make pregnancy look quite so chic! We did this mini-shoot just in time to capture some of the vibrant London fall foliage right in my own garden, and were lucky enough to catch a gorgeous sunny day. I’m So happy to have been able to document this special fleeting period in this momma’s life, and am looking forward to getting more of the new baby to come!
