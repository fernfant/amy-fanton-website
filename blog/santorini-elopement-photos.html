A Santorini Elopement

22 June 2017

I always feel so honoured when another photographer entrusts me with the job of documenting their special day. It means they really appreciate photography themselves, and they are almost always willing to go the extra mile to make sure their images are spectacular–whether that means waking up at sunrise to get the most beautiful light, choosing wardrobe carefully, or hiking for just a bit longer to find the perfect spot.

This couple was no exception and I had a wonderful time with them when they invited me to photograph their elopement on the beautiful island of Santorini. Before the elopement, we spent a beautiful evening exploring the island together and making the most of it’s dramatic and stunning landscape. It was so nice to have the entire beach to ourselves so that the couple could freely play and connect while I photographed them together. I’ll be back in for some Santorini wedding photography in just a few months, and I’m already counting the days!
