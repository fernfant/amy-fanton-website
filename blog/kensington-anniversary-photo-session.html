London Anniversary Photo Session in Kensington

1 October 2015

Just how adorable are these two?? I was so excited when I was initially contacted by this groom to schedule a a surprise anniversary photo session for his bride, commemorating their five year anniversary. It was a complete surprise for her and this just one part of a full day of activities he had organized to make the day special.

Do they come any more romantic??? We walked around the streets of Kensington where the couple laughed, connected and reflected on all of the wonderful time they’ve spent together. I feel so lucky to have helped you two document such a happy milestone and I wish you many more happy years to come!! :)
