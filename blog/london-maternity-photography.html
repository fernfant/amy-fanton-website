Newborn & Maternity

London Maternity Photography in Hyde Park

5 August 2014

I am so excited to share this London Maternity Photography Session with all of you! I met this beautiful family shortly after moving to London and was so excited when they asked me to do a family photo session! They are expecting a new baby in just a few months which makes my job even more fun because it means I was able to do maternity photography and family photography all in one session.

We spent a couple of minutes in our garden before heading over to the flower meadow at Kensington Palace. The resulting images have very different feels–the strong architecture of the buildings in our garden providing a nice contrast to the more romantic and dreamy field of grass. I love doing maternity sessions in wild, natural settings because I think it creates a very organic feel that works perfectly for pregnancy photos so those are definitely my favorite. It’s kind of hard to believe these were all taken in the center of London, isn’t it? Gosh I love this city!
