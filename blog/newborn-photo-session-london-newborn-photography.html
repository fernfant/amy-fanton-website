Beautiful Newborn Photo Session in Hampstead London

1 November 2014

I had the pleasure of photographing this sweet newborn session several weeks back in Hampstead London. This perfect baby was an angel during almost all of our session so after taking some lifestyle style newborn photos we decided to have some fun posing her amongst her little stuffed dolls.

Both of her grandmothers happened to be in town for the newborn photo session so in addition to photographing the nuclear family and the baby alone, I captured the three generations together and some intimate grandparent-grandchild shots. I was so happy to hear that the family made several canvases from the images as gifts and that the grandmother was delighted and emotional upon receiving such a sentimental token.

Towards the end of the session, I held the baby tight and rocked her to sleep before passing her off to her dad. She was such a warm little bundle of cuteness, it really makes me want another! I’ve seen several photos of the baby since the newborn photo session and it is amazing how quickly she is growing and changing–such a good reminder of how quickly this time passes and how lovely it is to document it before it is gone!
