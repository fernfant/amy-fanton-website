Hyde Park London Engagement Photo Session

15 October 2014

Super happy to share this gorgeous Engagement photo session with all of you! As I stepped off the tube on my way to this engagement photo session, I immediately noticed a gorgeous couple dressed to the nines and my first thought was “wow, I would love to photograph them”. I was beyond excited when after a few moments of walking in the same direction I realized that this beautiful couple actually were my engagement subjects!

I generally advise my clients to dress up a tad because I personally feel it results in a more polished product, but I was totally taken aback by the excellent wardrobe choices this couple made for their engagement photo session. The striking chiffon dress worn by the bride-to-be had so much beautiful movement in the breeze and the groom-to-be couldn’t have looked more sharp decked out in a bow-tie and tartan slacks.

We headed to Hyde Park where we split our time between a meadow of overgrown grasses and the Italian gardens so we could get a variety of engagement photos. I can’t decide which I like best–they feel so different to me! The couple were visiting from Seoul and I so wish they were getting married closer so that I could throw my name in the hat as a potential photographer for their wedding! Gah–I can only imagine how perfect it will be :)
