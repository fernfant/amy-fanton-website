Family

London Children's Portraits

5 March 2014

Yesterday afternoon I had the pleasure of photographing another of Oliver’s little friends during one of our play dates. This sweet little girl recently started taking ballet class and wanted to show us her beautiful ballerina “costume” and some of her newest dance moves (including being a butterfly and a swan). I happened to have my camera with me and couldn’t resist pulling it out for a few toddler portraits–she was just too sweet!

I decided to use the the first image below for this week’s project 52 submission with the theme “Soft”–although I think any of them could have worked! The soft light, the soft colors, her soft step…she was just so graceful and I think she makes the perfect ballerina, don’t you?
