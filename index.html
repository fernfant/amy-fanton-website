About

Hello, I’m Amy

A wedding photographer, an endless romantic, a perpetual optimist, and a lover of all things sparkly. I specialise in fine art wedding photography, shooting medium format film alongside digital to create images that are luminous, romantic, and natural.

My approach lets even the most camera-shy couples blossom in front of the lens. I treat ‘photo time’ as a chance for you to connect and simply enjoy one another — the camera fades away and authentic emotion takes over.

I’ve been honoured to have my work featured in Rock My Wedding, Love My Dress and others, and fortunate to photograph weddings across Spain, France, Italy, Greece, Morocco, Argentina, the Caribbean, the United States, and here in the UK.
