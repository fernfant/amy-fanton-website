# SEO & Link-Building Plan — Amy Fanton Photography

_Goal: recover Domain Rating (currently DR 9) and rank for winnable search terms._
_Ordered by impact ÷ effort — work top-down._

## Current state (baseline)

- **Domain Rating: 9** — modest, but recovering from past site downtime/suspension.
- **Backlinks: 154** (~42% dofollow). Profile punches well above DR 9:
  - Martha Stewart (DR 88), Elle Decor (DR 85), One Fab Day (DR 71),
    Style Me Pretty, Love My Dress, Rock My Wedding.
- On-page SEO: 9/9 (titles, schema, sitemap, performance all pass).
- Old WordPress URLs now redirect to live pages (no more 404 equity leaks).
- GSC property is ~1 week old; the **domain** is aged with real history.

---

## Tier 1 — This month (highest ROI, fully in your control)

### 1. Google reviews — biggest local lever
- Finish the Google Business Profile phone/name fix first.
- Ask **every past client** for a Google review. Target **5–10 in 60 days**, then 1–2/month.
- Template: _"Hi [name] — so lovely capturing your day! If you have a moment, a quick
  Google review would mean the world: [link]"_
- Repeat on **Bridebook, Hitched, WeddingWire, Facebook** — review counts feed those rankings too.

### 2. Claim every free directory profile (easy backlinks + citations)
- UK wedding: **Hitched, Bridebook, Guides for Brides, Rock My Wedding directory, WeddingWire/The Knot**
- General: **Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Yell**
- Use identical **Name / phone / website** everywhere (NAP consistency) and link to
  `https://www.fantonphotography.com`.

---

## Tier 2 — Reactivate what you already have

### 3. Re-engage publications that already featured you
- Submit your **best recent real wedding** to Love My Dress / Rock My Wedding / Style Me Pretty.
  Each acceptance = a fresh DR-70+ editorial backlink.
- Fill in the website field on the LMD supplier profile & SMP vendor page (currently missing the link).

### 4. Supplier cross-links (under-used goldmine)
- For every wedding, the **venue, florist, planner, dress designer, stationer** often has a
  "preferred suppliers" / "as featured" page.
- Email each: _"Loved working with you on [couple]'s day — here are the gallery images, free to
  use with a credit + link to fantonphotography.com."_ You give free photos; they give a relevant backlink.

---

## Tier 3 — Ongoing (compounds slowly)

### 5. Styled shoots
- Join/organise a styled shoot designed for publication. One published shoot = a backlink **plus**
  the whole vendor team crediting/linking you.

### 6. Long-tail journal posts
- Keep publishing venue-specific posts ("A Wedding at [Venue]"). Each can rank for that venue's
  searches while authority builds.

---

## What NOT to do
- ❌ No paid link schemes, Fiverr backlinks, or spammy directory blasts — Google penalises these.
- ❌ Don't change the homepage URL or anchor patterns — your best links point at the homepage; keep it stable.

---

## Realistic trajectory
- **Weeks:** Tier 1 (reviews + directories) can surface in local results.
- **2–4 months:** DR recovers as Google re-crawls existing famous links + the new redirects.
- **12 months+:** the competitive head term ("London wedding photographer"), won mainly via Tiers 2–3.

## Measurement
- `tools/rank_tracker.py <GSC-Queries.csv>` — track target-keyword positions over time.
- `tools/check_backlinks.py <Ahrefs-backlinks.csv>` — flag any backlink target that 404s.
- Watch **GSC impressions trend** (not clicks) as the first sign of progress.
